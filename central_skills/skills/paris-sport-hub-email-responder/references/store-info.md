# Paris Sport Hub — Store Reference

## About
Paris Sport Hub is a premium sporting goods store in the heart of Paris, specializing in tennis equipment, golf clubs, and athletic footwear for all skill levels. Expert staff provide personalized advice.

## Store Details
- **Address:** 15 Rue de Rivoli, 75001 Paris, France
- **Phone:** +33 1 23 45 67 89
- **Wheelchair Access:** Yes
- **Opening Hours:** Mon–Fri 09:00–19:30 | Sat 10:00–18:00 | Sunday: Closed

## Services
- Equipment Sales (Tennis, Golf, Footwear)
- Racket Stringing
- Golf Club Fitting (Right-Handed & Left-Handed)
- Click & Collect
- Returns & Exchanges
